Master's Student in Management Science and Engineering
School of Management, University of Science and Technology of China

I am a master's student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), advised by Prof. Hefu Liu. My research lies at the intersection of information systems and artificial intelligence, with a focus on multimodal learning and predictive analytics for digital platforms.

My current work studies how campaign text and images contribute to pre-launch crowdfunding prediction as their relationship with outcomes changes across environments. I also work on computational measurement of organizational culture. I am preparing for PhD study beginning in Fall 2027.

Research

My broader interest is computational design science in information systems. I develop and evaluate computational methods for problems involving heterogeneous information, changing environments, and constructs that require measurement from text.

Research interests

  • AI and analytics for digital platforms
  • Computational measurement of cultural and organizational constructs
  • Multimodal and multi-view representation learning
  • Temporal generalization under environmental change

Current directions

My crowdfunding research combines multimodal learning with temporal adaptation for pre-launch prediction. In a separate project, I use an established organizational-culture scale to anchor neural measurement of firms' competing values in annual-report text.

Working papers and research in progress

  1. Multimodal Learning under Evolving Environments for Crowdfunding Outcome Prediction

    Yihao Xu, Wenxiang Zhao, and Hefu Liu. Work in progress; planned submission to Information Systems Research in 2026.

    This study predicts crowdfunding outcomes before launch from campaign text and images as the relationship between project content and success changes over time. It combines multimodal representation learning with temporal adaptation. On 54,306 Kickstarter campaigns across four categories, its AUROC exceeded the strongest external benchmark in each category by 3.55–5.63 percentage points, using validation-based model selection, a fixed chronological split, and 20 training seeds per category against nine external methods.

  2. From Anchor to Evidence: A Theory-Driven Model for Newly Launched Crowdfunding Success Prediction

    Wenxiang Zhao, Yihao Xu, and Xiaoyu Yao. Working paper.

    Models how backers form an initial anchor and revise it using evidence available when a crowdfunding project launches.

  3. Scale-Anchored Neural Measurement of Firms' Competing Values

    Research in progress; measurement stage completed.

    Uses a 96-sentence scale-anchored instrument to measure Competing Values Framework orientations in approximately 4.37 million MD&A segments from 2001–2025.
