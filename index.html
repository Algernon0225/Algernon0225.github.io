Yihao Xu

Yihao's research interests span both the industrial and cultural fields. He is skilled at observing different scenarios from a unique perspective and designing scenario-specific algorithms to solve practical problems. For instance, in industrial fault diagnosis, he considers the multisensor characteristics of data sources and the advantages of joint optimization algorithms, and designs multisensor deep clustering networks (MsDCNs) for fault diagnosis. In the scenario of crowdfunding prediction, he starts from cultural value and designs a Cultural Value-based Prediction Model (CV-PM) based on Throsby's cultural value framework to extract the project's cultural value to predict success or failure, and also explores the mechanism of cultural influence on products. Beyond design science paradigms in scientific research, he also explores empirical research paradigms. In his recent research, he uses seed sentences and text embeddings to measure cultural matching between firms from annual reports and examines its relationship with supply chain efficiency and resilience.

Current work

His current manuscript introduces a cultural value perspective on pre-launch crowdfunding success prediction. It examines how diverse cultural attributes in campaign text and images jointly inform funding prospects and how their predictive relevance changes as crowdfunding environments evolve. SSC-TAP integrates complementary multimodal information and adapts predictions to the campaign’s content and launch time. Submission to Information Systems Research is planned for 2026.
