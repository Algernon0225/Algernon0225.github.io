I am a master's student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), advised by Prof. Hefu Liu. My research focuses on multimodal learning and predictive analytics for digital platforms.

I study how text and images can be combined for prediction as the relationship between content and outcomes changes over time. I also work on computational measurement of organizational culture. My first-author publications include a conference paper at ICIS 2025 and an article in IEEE Sensors Journal.

Research

My work takes a computational design science approach to information systems. I build and evaluate learning methods for prediction and measurement, with a focus on how information is represented and combined.

Research interests

  • AI and analytics for digital platforms
  • Computational measurement of cultural and organizational constructs
  • Multimodal and multi-view representation learning
  • Temporal generalization under environmental change

Current directions

Crowdfunding provides a setting for studying how project text and images inform predictions before fundraising begins, and how their predictive value changes over time. My culture-measurement work uses established survey items to measure firms’ competing values from annual-report text.

Publications

Peer-reviewed publications

  1. Subtly There, Deeply Felt: Modeling Cultural Values in Crowdfunding Success Prediction PDF

    Yihao Xu, Wenxiang Zhao, and Xiaoyu Yao.

    Proceedings of the 46th International Conference on Information Systems (ICIS), 2025. Best Complete Paper Nominee / Best-in-Track Paper.

    Represents Throsby’s six cultural values in project metadata, text, and images for crowdfunding success prediction. On 14,539 Kickstarter projects, the model achieved AUCs of 94.72% in Art and 95.14% in Design, exceeding the strongest baseline in each category by 1.49–3.65 percentage points.

  2. Multisensor Deep Clustering Networks With Joint Learning Strategy for Fault Diagnosis Considering Consensus-Specific Constraint PDF

    Yihao Xu, Mingfeng Lu, Gang Wang, and Fang Fang.

    IEEE Sensors Journal, 25(11), 19921–19936, 2025. doi:10.1109/JSEN.2025.3560460.

    Jointly trains feature extraction and clustering while retaining shared and sensor-specific information. The model achieved the highest mean clustering accuracy among the tested methods on six gearbox and bearing datasets, with gains of 19.83–28.41 percentage points over K-means.

Working papers and research in progress

  1. Multimodal Learning under Evolving Environments for Crowdfunding Outcome Prediction

    Yihao Xu, Wenxiang Zhao, and Hefu Liu.

    Work in progress. Planned submission to Information Systems Research in 2026.

    Combines multimodal representation learning with temporal adaptation for pre-launch crowdfunding prediction. Evaluation covers 54,306 Kickstarter campaigns in four categories, using a fixed chronological split and 20 training seeds per category. With validation-based model selection, AUROC improved by 3.55–5.63 percentage points over the strongest of nine benchmarks in each category.

  2. From Anchor to Evidence: A Theory-Driven Model for Newly Launched Crowdfunding Success Prediction

    Wenxiang Zhao, Yihao Xu, and Xiaoyu Yao. Working paper.

    Uses anchoring-and-adjustment theory to model the progression from project summaries to detailed information in crowdfunding prediction. The model reached AUCs of 94.57% on 13,192 Technology projects and 83.30% on 19,744 Art projects, outperforming the reported baselines.

  3. Scale-Anchored Neural Measurement of Firms' Competing Values

    Research in progress; measurement stage completed.

    Adapts OCAI-based culture measurement to annual-report text while retaining the survey’s item structure. The completed measurement stage has produced firm-year scores for four Competing Values Framework orientations from approximately 4.37 million MD&A segments spanning 2001–2025.
