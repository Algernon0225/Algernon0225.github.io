ICIS 2025

Subtly There, Deeply Felt: Modeling Cultural Values in Crowdfunding Success Prediction

Yihao Xu, Wenxiang Zhao, and Xiaoyu Yao

Proceedings of the 46th International Conference on Information Systems, 2025.

Best Complete Paper Nominee / Best-in-Track Paper

This study models cultural values in crowdfunding content and examines their roles in predicting project success. The Cultural Value-based Prediction Model (CV-PM) organizes metadata, text, and images around Throsby's six cultural values, refines the features with convolutional layers, and combines them through attention. On 14,539 Kickstarter projects, it exceeded the strongest baseline AUC by 1.49–3.65 percentage points. Value ablations showed the largest accuracy losses for social value in Art and aesthetic value in Design.

Multisensor Deep Clustering Networks With Joint Learning Strategy for Fault Diagnosis Considering Consensus-Specific Constraint

Yihao Xu, Mingfeng Lu, Gang Wang, and Fang Fang

IEEE Sensors Journal, 25(11), 19921–19936, 2025.

This study addresses two problems in unsupervised fault diagnosis: separately trained features may not suit clustering, and multisensor data contain both shared and sensor-specific information. The multisensor deep clustering network (MsDCN) combines a sensor-aware attentive encoder with sensor-specific encoders and jointly optimizes reconstruction, clustering, and consensus-specific objectives. It achieved the highest mean clustering accuracy on six gearbox and bearing datasets, with gains of 5.74–11.35 percentage points over the strongest comparison method on the two DIRG bearing datasets.

Multimodal Learning under Evolving Environments for Crowdfunding Outcome Prediction

Yihao Xu, Wenxiang Zhao, and Hefu Liu

Work in progress. Planned submission to Information Systems Research in 2026.

Campaign text and images convey both overlapping and complementary information. SSC-TAP organizes shared, modality-specific, and interaction information within a common semantic reference, then learns their joint contribution to pre-launch prediction.

Historical content–outcome relationships can change as crowdfunding environments evolve. Temporal relevance adaptation separates changes in content distributions from changes in predictive relevance, adjusting content contributions for each new campaign and its actual launch time.

Along Liu, Mengru Zhang, Yihao Xu, and Hefu Liu

Research in progress.

Investigates cultural complementarity in the efficiency–resilience trade-off. A scale-anchored text measurement protocol combines semantic retrieval, expert-reviewed annual-report examples, and item-level scoring.

Links firm-level cultural profiles to disclosed supplier and customer relationships, constructing transaction-weighted matching measures for annual panel and disruption–recovery analyses.

Knowing When to Be Firm: Communication Style Shifts in AI-Assisted Food Delivery

Early-stage Research Project.

Food-delivery customers may have explicit coupon requests alongside unspoken emotional needs. This project examines shifts between assertive and nonassertive AI language during coupon assistance and emotional support, tracing customer responses across conversational turns and preference profiles to study subsequent purchase decisions.
